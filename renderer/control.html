Why screenshots and not watching the screen: when you press the game's Screenshot key, Tarkov names the file with your exact position and facing, for example …_-118.23, 1.91, 227.54_0.0, 0.93, 0.0, 0.36_…png. The app reads that name, deletes the file, and moves your dot. Watching the screen cannot give coordinates at all: the game shows no minimap and no numbers, so a vision model could only guess "near Dorms", slowly, and it would cost money per frame. The filename is exact, free and instant. Nothing reads the game's memory or touches its files.

In EFT ▸ Settings ▸ Controls ▸ Screenshot, bind the key below.

A mouse button is only watched, so Tarkov still gets the click. The hold-to-stream key above is different: the app only watches it, Tarkov still gets it.

1 · What it is, what it is not, and why screenshots

TarkovMap reads two things the game writes to your own PC: the log files in the EFT folder (which map, when the raid started, quest started/finished) and the names of in-game screenshots, which the game stamps with your position and facing. From those it draws a live minimap, a full map, quest objectives, extract distances and, for friends who also run it, their positions. It never reads game memory, never injects anything, and never shows enemies.

Why the screenshot key and not "watching the screen": the filename carries your exact coordinates; the screen carries none. Tarkov has no minimap and no position readout, so a vision model looking at your monitor could only guess where you are, slowly and at a cost per frame. Reading a filename is exact, instant and free. The PNG is deleted about a second after it appears, so nothing piles up.

2 · First run in five steps

  1. Setup ▸ Game: the EFT folder is detected from Steam or the BSG launcher. Fix it if wrong.
  2. In EFT ▸ Settings ▸ Controls, bind Screenshot to the key shown in Setup ▸ Position feed (default F11).
  3. Pick a mode: Hold (hold Caps Lock in raid and the dot streams), Auto (every 2 s in raid) or Manual.
  4. Press Test, go into a raid (offline works), tap the screenshot key once. The test shows your coordinates.
  5. Save. The overlay appears on your main screen; the full map on the second one if you have it.

3 · Hotkeys

All five are changeable in Setup ▸ Hotkeys, and each can be switched off. Defaults:

  • F7 / F8 map opacity down / up
  • F9 make the overlay clickable (again to hand the mouse back)
  • F10 hide / show the overlay
  • F6 drop a "regroup" ping at your position for the squad
  • Caps Lock (changeable in Setup ▸ Position feed) hold to stream your position — only in Hold mode; the default Auto mode streams by itself whenever Tarkov or Arena is the front window

They must not collide with your Tarkov binds. A hotkey the app registers is taken system-wide: Windows hands it to TarkovMap and Tarkov never sees the press. The app cannot read your Tarkov binds (the game keeps them on its server, not in a file), so check them yourself once. The hold-to-stream key and the screenshot key are different: the app only watches or sends those, Tarkov still receives them.

4 · The overlay

The Raid page opens with Is it working?: every link of the chain (key helper, Tarkov running and in front, the Screenshot key read from the game's own log, presses, screenshot files, position, map from the log, minimap and full map windows) with a tick, a cross or a dot, refreshed every second, and a button that presses once and reports where the chain stops. The same rows are written to health.json in the data folder every 5 s. The app reads Tarkov's Make screenshot bind from the game's log and follows it, so the key never has to be set by hand — with one exception it cannot fix: Print Screen, which Windows hands to Snipping Tool before any game sees it; rebind Make screenshot to F11 in Tarkov, or switch the Print screen shortcut off in Windows Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Keyboard.

You do not start TarkovMap yourself: it registers as a Windows login item (Setup ▸ Game) and waits in the tray. When Tarkov or Arena is running the full map appears on the second monitor; when the game is the active window the overlay appears. When the game closes both go away again.

By default the overlay shows only while Escape from Tarkov or Arena is the active window (the "only while Tarkov is in front" chip on the Raid page); switch it off to keep the overlay on the desktop too. Closing the settings window with its X quits the whole app; use the tray to reopen it while it runs.

While the left or right mouse button is held with Tarkov in front — aiming or firing — the minimap, panel and tags disappear and come back on release ("hide while aiming / firing" on the Raid page). The app never presses F12, Steam's screenshot key. If presses go out and no screenshot appears, the app rotates through five ways of synthesising the key (8 presses each) until the game answers; the health card shows the style in use.

The minimap sits in the corner you choose and turns with you (heading-up) so "ahead" is always up. Rings mark 50 and 100 m. Next to it: quests on this map with the trader's portrait and the distance, extracts and teammates sorted by distance with a direction glyph, and the raid line with the timer. It is click-through, so it never eats a click meant for the game, until you press F9. All sizes and opacities live on the Raid page.

5 · The full map

Opens on the second monitor (or with the "Open full map" button). Same imagery and markers, every layer, floors, the flea slider and the ask box. Drag and zoom freely; the minimap is a window onto this map.

6 · The map imagery and aligning a render

Three looks. Light (default) and Dark are drawn by this app from the game's vector data — every map, every floor, one floor at a time; Labyrinth and Icebreaker were traced from the game's own tiles because no vector of them exists anywhere. 3D is RE3MR's render (reemr.se, Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0; his credit stays on the map) on the maps that have one (the Map page lists them). Aligning a render means telling the app where game coordinates land on the picture: on the Map page, click the same spot on the render and on the tiles, six or more pairs across the map, and save. The error shown is the average miss in metres; under five is good. Every map ships pre-aligned by me, so this is only for nudging.

7 · Quests

The page has two tabs. My progression is the dashboard: a ring with the share of quests done and your phase of the wipe, Kappa and Lightkeeper cards with how many quests each still needs and how many must be finished one after another, a ring around every trader's portrait for that trader's line, then your accepted quests as cards (biggest unlocks first) and what is ready to accept. Every quest carries one line on why it matters: the trader it unlocks, whether it is on the road to Kappa or Lightkeeper, and what it opens. All quests lists everything grouped by trader, with state chips, a map filter and a search box.

When the game says a quest started, it appears; when the game says it finished, it disappears. Every start reads your whole log history again, so a quest you finished while the app was closed is picked up. Objectives with a fixed spot get a marker with the trader's face; hover or click one on the full map for the quest's state, level, objectives and a wiki button. Visit and extract objectives tick themselves when your position enters the zone; item counts and kills are not in the logs, so tick those in the popup or on the Quests page. The All quests layer draws every quest on the map: amber accepted, white not started, grey locked behind earlier quests, green done (hidden unless "show finished" is on). The Quest items layer marks items that accepted and future quests need, at the spots where they can spawn, with an importance score you filter with the slider; quest-specific items (documents, drives) lie at their exact spots. Your level is not in the logs, so "not started" only means the earlier quests are done.

8 · Squad sharing and pings

Everyone types the same squad code and their name. On the same network the app finds the others by itself; positions are shared only inside the same raid. Teammates show on both maps and as a small name tag in the game view (approximate, refreshed with each of your fixes). Pings mark a spot at your position with a word; they show on every squadmate's maps and as a tag, and expire after five minutes.

9 · Layers, flea filter and the ask box

Every layer chip has an eye next to it: green eye = drawn on the in-game minimap too, red crossed eye = full map only. "Hide all in game" clears the minimap to just you, your squad and the raid line in one click.

Chips switch layers per map: extracts, quests, all quests, quest items, places, distance text, squad, keys, bosses, scav spawns, PMC spawns, hazards, containers, loot, machine guns, switches. Loot value (Style, or the Layers page) colours 25 m squares by the loot that can spawn there. The flea slider lights loot spots where an item worth at least that much can spawn (a spawn map, not what actually spawned). The ask box takes a sentence like show extracts, dorm keys and anything over 40k or quest items and all quests; if the built-in parser doesn't get it and you added an OpenRouter key, a free model translates it. Marker data comes from json.tarkov.dev; the game's own data covers spawns, bosses and names when it is down, and the app retries every 15 minutes.

10 · Fullscreen vs borderless

The minimap is a transparent window on top of the desktop, so it can only be seen where Windows composes the desktop. Borderless always works. Fullscreen works on Windows 11 as long as "fullscreen optimizations" are on for EscapeFromTarkov.exe (the default; check the exe's Properties ▸ Compatibility tab that "Disable fullscreen optimizations" is unticked). True exclusive fullscreen hands the screen to the game alone and nothing outside the game can draw over it — the only way in would be injecting into the game, which this app will never do. The app re-asserts the overlay on top every time Tarkov becomes the active window; if you still see nothing, switch to borderless (no performance difference on Windows 11) or use the second-monitor map, which always works.

11 · Privacy, storage and fair play

Screenshots are deleted about a second after they appear; the folder count on the Raid page should read zero. Map tiles and RE3MR renders are cached once under the data folder and can be cleared; your quest book and ticked objectives live there too (quests.json, quest-progress.json). Nothing is uploaded except your squad messages to your own squad and the ask-box sentence to OpenRouter when you enabled it. No enemy positions, no memory reading, no injection.

12 · Troubleshooting

  • No fix: the screenshot key is not bound in EFT, or the mode is Manual. Press Test and tap the key.
  • Wrong map / "in menu" forever: the EFT folder path is wrong; check that build\Logs exists inside it. Pick a map by hand on the Raid page to plan.
  • Overlay invisible: see section 10. Press F10 once in case it was hidden.
  • Teammates missing: same squad code, same raid, same network; Windows Firewall must allow the app on private networks.
  • Blank map or empty layers: the render is still downloading or slicing (Map page shows progress), tiles are still downloading (Setup ▸ Storage), or tarkov.dev is down (red notice; retries every 15 min).
